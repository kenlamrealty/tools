            Next steps

            1. Pick the right pro: legal questions (title, trust, disputes) go to a lawyer, tax questions to a CPA or EA. Big sales often need both.
            2. Verify on State Bar, CBA or the IRS Directory before the first meeting.
            3. Start cheap: a county bar referral is 30 minutes for $40–45. Get the fee in writing and enter the quotes here.
            4. Bring your numbers: do I need a lawyer (T120), trust vs will (T121), capital gains on a sale (T22), 1031 (T27).

            Common misconception: "My escrow officer or agent can tell me how to hold title or what tax I owe." They cannot give legal or tax advice. How you take title (joint tenancy, community property, trust) changes taxes and probate later. A 30-minute county bar referral ($40–45) and a CPA call before closing cost far less than fixing it after.
