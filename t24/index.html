Next steps

  1. Do the "Do" items first, cheapest-with-biggest-return first: clean, declutter, paint, yard. They also make the photos.
  2. Order a pre-listing home and termite inspection. Fix the small stuff now; you will disclose the rest anyway. Check the pest company license at pestboard.ca.gov.
  3. Check every contractor at cslb.ca.gov before paying. Get bids with the repair cost tool.
  4. Follow the full prep timeline in the pre-sale checklist, then run your net in the seller net sheet.

Many people get this wrong: "Remodel the kitchen and I'll get it back." Usually not. In San Francisco-area data, a $97,667 midrange kitchen remodel adds about $47,360 at resale (48.5%). Paint, clean and a garage door often return more than they cost.
