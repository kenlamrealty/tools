Next steps

  1. Open the boundary tool for each district above and type the exact street address.
  2. Santa Clara County home? Check the Assessor's “School District/Tax Rate Area” tab too. Two sources, same answer, you're good.
  3. Call the enrollment office before your investigation period ends. Ask if the school has room for your grade.
  4. Compare the schools you found with T141, then run the monthly cost with T01.

Many people get this wrong: “the closest school is my school.” Not always. Your school is set by the district's boundary map, and boundaries can zig-zag down the middle of a street. A full school can also send new kids to another campus.
