A common misunderstanding: “closing costs are the money I bring to escrow.” Not quite. Cash to close = total closing costs + down payment − deposit − seller credits ± adjustments such as tax and HOA prorations. The Loan Estimate shows it in a separate table (“Calculating Cash to Close”, 12 CFR 1026.37(h)). Seller credits also have limits: on a conventional loan for a primary home, 3%, 6% or 9% depending on the down payment, never more than your actual closing costs, and never as cash back (Fannie Mae).
