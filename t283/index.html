Issues to know
Ranges are estimates, not quotes. Before you remove contingencies, get an as-built quote from a designer and a contractor, and ask the city about fees.

A common mistake: "Legalizing only costs the permit fee." No. You pay for as-built plans, opening walls for inspection, and correcting anything that does not meet current code. For an ADU the total can run $20K to $200K or more. The permit fee is the small part.
