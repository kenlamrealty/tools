Next steps

  1. Ask the park manager today for the park rules, a blank rental agreement and the last 3 years of rent increase notices. Read them before you fall for the home.
  2. Run the real monthly number (loan + space rent + tax + insurance) in T145, and check how fast space rent can rise in T162.

  4. Words you don't know (decal, 433A, MRL, EBS)? Look them up in T176.

Many people get this wrong: "the seller said yes, so the home is mine." Not in a park. You also need the park's approval and a signed rental agreement. Without a signed rental agreement the law says you have no right to stay on the space (Civil Code §798.75). Buy the home, lose the land, and you're stuck.
