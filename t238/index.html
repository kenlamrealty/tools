Owed whether you claimed the depreciation or not ("allowed or allowable"). Any loss carried forward is released at sale and lowers this. Price gain is taxed on top — see T222.

Common mistake: "I'll skip depreciation so I don't pay it back later." The IRS taxes the depreciation you could have taken when you sell, claimed or not. Skip it and you lose the deduction now and still pay the 25% later. Take it every year.
