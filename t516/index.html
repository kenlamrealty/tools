BIA: căn nhà không ai bảo đảm, mình phải tự kiểm

Seller không bảo đảm. Agent cũng không. BIA là tờ giấy nói thẳng chuyện đó, rồi liệt kê 14 nhóm thứ anh chị nên cho người có nghề kiểm trước khi mua.

Đọc form từng đoạn

Bấm số đỏ trên tờ giấy hoặc trong danh sách. Chữ xanh là ví dụ điền vào. Đoạn 3 có 14 mục A–N, em gom lại thành 7 nhóm.

Buyer's Investigation AdvisoryC.A.R. Form BIA
11. Why investigate. Neither Seller nor Brokers guarantee the condition. Buyer must use reasonable care. Broker only does a limited visual look (AVID). Hire pros, get written reports, follow up on their advice.
22. Broker limits. Brokers are not experts in these items. Referrals carry no guarantee.
33. Strongly advised to investigate — or you act against Brokers' advice.
A. General condition: foundation, roof, plumbing, heating, A/C, electrical, pool/spa, built-ins, energy efficiency.
4B. Square footage, age, boundaries — numbers are approximations only. Fences ≠ true lines. Listing: 1,650 sq ft · built 1962
5C. Wood destroying pests · D. Soil stability (fill, expansive soil, drainage)
6E. Water, well, sewer/septic · L. Utilities & internet
7F. Environmental hazards (asbestos, lead, radon, mold, tanks…) · G. Earthquake & flood
8H. Insurance — availability and cost vary; shop early, before removing loan/insurance contingencies.
9I. Permits, zoning, address — mailing city may not be the city in charge. · J. Rent limits · K. Pool & safety rules
10M. Solar (owned, leased, financed, PPA) · N. Neighborhood & personal factors
