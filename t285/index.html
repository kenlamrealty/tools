Is this permit still open, or expired?

Enter the dates from the city portal to see whether the permit is still active, how many days remain, and who should resolve it before close.

What to do now

    A common mistake: "The work is done, so an expired permit does not matter." It does. Without a final inspection, the city never signed off. Reopening it means new work must meet current code, not the code from the year the permit was issued.
