Your rights when you apply

  • Application fee: max $68.96 per adult in 2026. The cap goes up with inflation each year.
  • You get a receipt that lists what the fee paid for. Ask, and you get a copy of your credit report.
  • No unit available? They can't charge the fee. Unused part of the fee comes back to you.
  • Deposit: since 7/1/2024, most landlords can take only 1 month. Up to 2 months only for a small landlord (a person, 2 properties or less, 4 units or less), and never from active-duty service members.

Next steps

  1. Pull your free credit report at annualcreditreport.com before you apply. Fix errors first.
  2. Have it ready: 2 recent pay stubs, last year's W-2 or tax return, ID, 2 months of bank statements. Good applicants move fast.
  3. Read the state's Landlord-Tenant Guide and rent increase rules in T55.
  4. Paying this much rent anyway? Compare with buying in T04 — rent vs buy.

Many people get this wrong: “The landlord can ask for 2–3 months deposit.” Not anymore. Since July 1, 2024 (AB 12), most landlords can take only 1 month plus first month's rent. Small landlords can take up to 2. Nobody can take 3.
