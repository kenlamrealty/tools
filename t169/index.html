Next steps

  1. This week, send the park a short written request for its list of required repairs on sale. The 15-business-day clock starts when the park receives it.
  2. See what you would keep after commission, payoff and fees, and whether selling to the park makes sense, in T168.
  3. Has the owner passed away? Start with T172 before listing. Is the park resisting the sale? See T171.
  4. Locate the HCD title, the latest registration card and the most recent tax bill now. Missing papers are the most common cause of delay.

A common misconception: "The park can make me fix anything before I sell." It cannot. On a sale, the park may require only exterior repairs that a law, an ordinance or a park rule based on one requires, the same items it could demand at any other time (Civil Code §798.73.5). Ask for the list in writing. If the park fails to provide it within 15 business days of your request, it waives those repairs as a condition of sale, except items required by health and safety laws.
