Next steps

  1. Set a real budget first with T02. The max price you type here should come from that.
  2. Pick 2–3 ZIPs and look at actual homes on search.kenlamrealty.com.
  3. Check the school for each address, not the city. Boundaries split ZIPs. School tool: T06, or ask the district.
  4. Drive the area on a weekday at 8 AM and on a Saturday. Traffic and parking look different.

Many people get this wrong: "the median is what a house costs there." It's the middle of all sales that month, condos and townhomes included. A single-family home often costs more, and in small ZIPs the median can jump 10% to 40% month to month.
