What restarts the 3-day wait

  1. The APR increases by more than 1/8 of a percentage point (1/4 for irregular loans, such as many ARMs). A decrease does not count.
  2. The loan product changes, for example from fixed to adjustable.
  3. A prepayment penalty is added.

Any other change, such as a fee changing, only requires a corrected CD at or before closing, with no new wait. After closing: a corrected CD within 30 days if a change affects what you paid, and within 60 days for a non-numeric clerical error.

Our own walkthrough of the five-page Closing Disclosure, not the official form. Section names follow 12 CFR 1026.38. The rule does not number the pages or letter Sections K–N; those labels follow the model form you usually see (H-25).
