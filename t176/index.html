Many people get this wrong: "mobile home" and "modular home" are the same thing. They're not. A mobile or manufactured home has a steel frame underneath, an HCD title and, in a park, no land. A modular home is built to the normal building code, sits on a foundation and is sold like a regular house with a grant deed. The loan, the tax and the resale are all different.
