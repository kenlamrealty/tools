Next steps

    Many people get this wrong

    "I'll look at permits after the inspection." Too late, often. In San Jose, copies of documents take 7–10 business days and copies of plans 30–40 days, and the owner has to sign. Ask on day one. If the papers can't come back before your deadline, ask for more time in writing before it runs out.

    "The inspector checks permits." A home inspector looks at the house. The permit history lives at the city. Nobody pulls it for you unless you ask, and your agent's walk-through does not cover public records or permits either (Civ. Code 2079.3).

    "Once I tick everything, I'm covered." The ticks are your notes, not a legal shield. They do help: a dated record shows what you checked and when. Keep it with your transaction papers.
