A couple’s birth years and buying a home

Enter both birth dates and the year you plan to buy to see your East/West groups, shared favorable directions, how your birth years match, and who should lead the ceremony. Cultural reference, not financial or legal advice.

▾
Who leads the ceremony
—
Names on title (vesting) are a legal and tax decision, not one based on age or feng shui. Ask an attorney or CPA; escrow can explain the forms of vesting but cannot advise which to choose. Here, feng shui only decides who leads the ceremony.

Favorable directions for the home

A common misunderstanding: “One of us is East group and the other West, so we cannot buy.” Not true. Many families choose a facing based on one person, then each sets their bed and desk toward their own favorable direction.

How your birth years match

Tam hợp or lục hợp = a good match. Xung, hại or hình = take a little more care and talk things through. Cultural reference.

Year check

  • Tuổi mụ = year − lunar birth year + 1. Kim Lâu: tuổi mụ ÷ 9 leaves a remainder of 1, 3, 6 or 8. Hoang Ốc: lands on house 3, 5 or 6. Tam Tai: by your branch group.
  • Same rules as tool T41. None = favorable · 1 = consider carefully · 2–3 = avoid holding the ceremony that year.
