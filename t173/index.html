Who issues the permit?

In most parks it's HCD (state). A city or county can take over park enforcement with HCD's approval. Look up your park in the HCD park search to see its enforcement agency, or call HCD Codes & Standards, 800-952-8356. Work on the home itself (water heater, furnace, re-roof, panel, plumbing) gets a permit before work starts, even like-for-like.

Next steps

  1. Apply first, fix later. Habitat sends staff to look at the home and sets the scope of work. Have ready: photo ID, HCD title, space lease, homeowner's insurance, income papers for every adult.
  2. Get 2 bids from contractors who do mobile homes (C-47 license for installation and leveling work). Ask each one: "Who pulls the permit, you or me?"
  3. Tell the park manager before work that changes the outside (skirting, steps, deck, roof). Park rules can set colors and materials.
  4. Selling soon? Fix the safety items first and keep permits and receipts. Buyers and lenders ask. See T169 and T168.

Many people get this wrong: "a water heater swap doesn't need a permit." In a mobile home it does, even like-for-like. HCD wants the permit, the manufacturer's specs and, for gas, the piping calcs. California also requires the tank to be strapped for earthquakes. Skip it and the buyer's inspector will flag it when you sell.
