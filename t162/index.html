Next steps

  1. Pull your last three increase notices. For each one: was there 90 days' notice, and is the percentage within the number above?
  2. Read the first page of your rental agreement. If it says the space is exempt from local rent control, call the city before you sign anything new.
  3. Increase looks too high? Call the city number above first. Many cities review an increase only if residents file within 30 days.
  4. Planning to sell? The rule on sale affects your price; run it in T168. Full monthly cost: T145. Something else seems off: T171. Park closing: T177.

A common misconception: "California caps mobile home space rent at 5%." It does only for a park located in two or more cities at once, which is rare. Everywhere else, the only cap is the city's own rule. No city rule, no cap.
