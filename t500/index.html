SXA: the seller wants to do a 1031 exchange

A one-page add-on to the purchase contract. The seller says "I plan to roll this sale into another property to defer tax," and the buyer agrees to help, at no cost and no risk.

Who gives it
The seller's side, usually with a counter offer
Who signs
Seller and buyer
When you see it
Selling an investment or rental property
Required?
No. Only if the seller wants a 1031 exchange
Pages
1 page, 8 short paragraphs
Version
SXA Reviewed 6/25 (current)

Version check: SXA was not on C.A.R.'s December 2025 or June 2026 lists of revised forms. "Reviewed 6/25" is still the current version. Its twin for buyers is the BXA.
