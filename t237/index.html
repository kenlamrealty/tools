The catch: the value comes back

Prop 8 is temporary. Your Prop 13 factored base keeps growing up to 2% a year. When prices recover, the Assessor can raise the value back up to that line, and in those years the jump can be more than 2%.

Common mistake: "Zillow says my home dropped, so my tax drops." The county does not watch Zillow. You file, or nothing happens. And the value that counts is January 1 — a price drop in March helps next year, not this one.
