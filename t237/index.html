Day 1 = the day of the first pull. We count it that way to stay on the safe side.
The window only covers mortgage, auto and student loan inquiries. A credit card application is its own inquiry.
Since Sep 9, 2026, any Fannie/Freddie lender may use VantageScore 4.0 instead of Classic FICO. VantageScore groups pulls in a 14-day window, so 14 days is the number to plan around.

Common myth: "Every lender I talk to knocks my score down again." Not if you keep the mortgage pulls close together. Inside 14 days, FICO and VantageScore treat them as one inquiry. Checking your own score is a soft pull and never counts.
