California rules that protect you

  1. Handyman: under $1,000 total, labor and materials together. Only if the job needs no permit, they hire no helpers, and it is not a bigger job split up. B&P §7048 · $1,000 since 1/1/2025
  2. Deposit: no more than 10% or $1,000, whichever is less. Pay the rest only as work gets done. B&P §7159 · CSLB
  3. 3 business days to cancel (5 if you are 65+) when you sign anywhere other than the contractor's place of business. Cancel in writing. Civil Code §1689.6 · CSLB
  4. Every change in writing. Get the "Notice to Owner" about mechanics liens. Ask for lien releases from subs and suppliers. CSLB
  5. Permits: the contract should say the contractor pulls them and the fee is in the price. Check what needs one at T92. CSLB
  6. A "B" general contractor can take a plumbing-only or electrical-only job only if they also hold that license, or the job has 2+ unrelated trades. B&P §7057
  7. The $25,000 license bond is not insurance. It is small. Ask for liability and workers' comp too. CSLB
