    Next steps

    1. List every asset and how it is titled. Your deed shows "joint tenancy", "community property" or a trust name. Check beneficiaries on 401k, IRA and life insurance too.
    2. Talk to a California attorney who does estate planning. Get a flat-fee quote in writing. No lawyer yet? Use a State Bar certified lawyer referral service. Santa Clara County Bar: 669-302-7803, 30 minutes for a $45 fee.
    3. A trust only works if the house is deeded into it. Ask the attorney to record the new deed, then check it on the county recorder site.
    4. Plan to leave the home to a child? Read the Prop 19 rules first: T25 Prop 19 parent-child. When to call a lawyer for other cases: T120.

    Common misconception: "Probate fees are based on my equity, and I still owe a lot, so it's cheap." Wrong. California fees are based on the gross value of what goes through probate. The mortgage is not subtracted. A $1.4M home with $450K owed is billed as $1.4M.
