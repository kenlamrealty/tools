Business days here: Monday–Friday, no federal holidays (assumes the lender's office is closed Saturday). The 7-day rule counts Saturdays. If the LE is mailed, count it as received 3 business days later.

6 things that make an "application"

  1. Your name
  2. Your income
  3. Your Social Security number (to pull credit)
  4. The property address
  5. An estimate of the home's value
  6. The loan amount you want

Once the lender has these 6, the 3-day clock starts. A lender can't make you send a purchase contract or pay stubs first just to give you a Loan Estimate.

A Loan Estimate is not an approval. At this point the lender has not approved or denied you. It only shows the terms you'd likely get if you move forward.
Before you say "intent to proceed": the only fee a lender may charge is a reasonable credit report fee. No application fee, no appraisal fee yet. Silence doesn't count as intent. You have to tell them.
