Next steps

  1. Ask the listing agent for the HOA budget, reserve study and any special assessment. A $400 HOA can become $600.
  2. Check the actual school for each address on the California School Dashboard, then confirm boundaries with the district. Listings are often wrong.
  3. Drive the commute at rush hour, not on a Sunday.
  4. Get one home's full breakdown in T01, then get pre-approved (T34) so your offer is ready.

Many people get this wrong: the lowest price is not always the cheapest home. HOA, property tax and insurance come every month. A $50K cheaper condo with a $650 HOA can cost more per month than the house.
