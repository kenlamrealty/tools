Can it be a 1031?
—
General information only, not tax or legal advice. The IRS looks at intent and all the facts. Talk with a CPA and your Qualified Intermediary before you list.

A common misunderstanding: "I rented my vacation home a few weekends, so it is a rental." Not by itself. The IRS safe harbor requires 24 months of ownership, at least 14 days a year rented at fair rent, and personal use of no more than 14 days or 10% of rented days.
