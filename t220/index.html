Can it be a 1031?
—
General information only, not tax or legal advice. The IRS looks at your intent and the full facts. Ask a CPA and your Qualified Intermediary before you list.

Common mistake: "I rented it a few weekends, so my vacation home is a rental." Not by itself. The IRS safe harbor wants 24 months of ownership, at least 14 days a year at fair rent, and personal use no more than 14 days or 10% of rented days.
