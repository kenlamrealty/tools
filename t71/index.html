Next steps

    
    
      
  1. For every Red item, get a specialist (roofer, structural engineer, sewer, electrician) to look and give a written bid before day 17.
    2. 
      
  2. Check the license before anyone works: contractors at cslb.ca.gov, termite companies at pestboard.ca.gov.
    3. 
      
  3. Send the seller only the Red items and the bigger Yellow ones, on a Request for Repair. Asking for everything makes the seller say no to everything.
    4. 
      
  4. Smell gas? Leave the house and call PG&E at 1-800-743-5000. The safety check is free.
    5. 
    

    
Many people get this wrong: "Every item on the report is a deal-breaker." No. Inspectors write down everything, so even newer homes get a long list. Most items are Green. Focus on the Red ones.
