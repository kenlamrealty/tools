Paperwork checklist

A common misunderstanding: "I moved to Texas and the replacement is in Texas, so I am done with California." No. California tracks the gain earned here. Form 3840 is due every year, even while you live out of state, and even after later exchanges from one other state to another. If you skip it, the FTB can assess tax plus penalties and interest.
