    About the “quẻ Kinh Dịch” method: it is popular for choosing phone numbers (split the digits, divide by 8, read the hexagram). It is rarely used for house numbers and is set up differently from place to place, so this tool does not calculate it.

    A common misunderstanding: Assuming a “bad” number lowers the price. Price comes from comps (recent comparable sales), location and condition. Where researchers have measured a number effect, it was small and appeared only in areas where many buyers avoid that number.
