Remodeling this year? Which side to leave undisturbed

Choose the year and month to see where the annual and monthly flying stars fall, and which side of the house is best left alone for digging or demolition. Cultural reference, not financial or legal advice.

▾

Side = direction measured from the center of the house. Stand in the middle of the house with a compass (tool T406).

Year {y} · cultural reference
—
Annual star chart (South on top)

South is on top, as in Vietnamese feng shui books. Large number = annual star. Small number at top right = monthly star.

A common misunderstanding: Ngũ Hoàng does not mean you cannot live there. A bedroom on that side can be used normally. Simply avoid demolition, drilling and digging there during the year. Many practitioners consider small repairs and interior paint acceptable.

What to do

    Folk remedies, not required: a six-rod metal wind chime or a brass gourd (hồ lô) on the Ngũ Hoàng or Nhị Hắc side; a glass of salt water (changed monthly); avoid red and earthy yellow there. Tam Sát: a Kỳ Lân or Tỳ Hưu set. Some practitioners say none of this is needed and that leaving the side undisturbed is enough.

    12 feng shui months

    Each month begins at a solar term (tiết), shown in California time. Ngũ Hoàng moves every month.
