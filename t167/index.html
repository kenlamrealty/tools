Next steps

  1. Found a park? Drive it on a weekday evening. Talk to a resident, not just the manager.
  2. Ask the park for the current space rent, the last 3 years of increases, and whether the space is on a long-term lease (those can be exempt from city rent rules). Run the numbers in T145 and T162.
  3. Senior park? Ask the exact age rule (55+ or 62+) and who else may live there. Then go through the buying checklist in T165.
  4. Check the home's age for loans and insurance in T166, and repair costs in T173.

Many people get this wrong: "the city has rent control, so my space is covered." Not always. In San Jose the ordinance covers spaces permitted before Sept 7, 1979. Long-term leases can be exempt in some cities. Sunnyvale and Campbell use agreements with parks, not a city law. Ask the city about your exact space before you buy.
