Photos that hold up

  1. Turn on the date and time stamp, or email the photos to yourself the same day so the email records the date.
  2. Walk through with one slow, continuous video. State the date and address aloud at the start.
  3. Take a wide shot of each room, then close-ups of every mark. Place a coin or key next to it for scale.
  4. Open the oven, refrigerator, dishwasher, cabinets and the space under the sinks. Photograph the meter readings and the keys you received.
  5. Repeat the same steps on move-out day, in the same order. Before-and-after photos side by side settle most disputes.
