Photos that hold up

  1. Turn on the date and time stamp, or email the photos to yourself the same day so the email shows the date.
  2. Walk through with one slow video. Say the date and the address out loud at the start.
  3. Take a wide shot of each room, then close-ups of every mark. Put a coin or a key next to it for size.
  4. Open the oven, fridge, dishwasher, cabinets and the space under sinks. Photograph meter readings and the keys you got.
  5. Do the same thing again on move-out day, in the same order. Before and after side by side wins arguments.
