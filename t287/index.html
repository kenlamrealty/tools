    Many people get this wrong: "The SPQ has a box for unpermitted work, so the seller already told me." Not quite. The SPQ (6/24) asks about alterations and repairs in general. Only a seller who bought within 18 months must list permits and unpermitted work. The TDS asks about work done without permits, but only what the seller knows. Ask in writing anyway.
