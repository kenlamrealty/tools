How it works

  • The year pillar changes at Lập Xuân (around Feb 4), not at Tết. The month pillar changes at each of the 12 solar terms (tiết). So Bát tự years and months can differ from the lunar calendar.
  • The day pillar follows the local birth date. The hour pillar uses two-hour blocks (Tý 23–1h, Sửu 1–3h…). Use the clock time where you were born. For US births, use local time, including daylight saving if it applied.
