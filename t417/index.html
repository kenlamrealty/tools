Repair or remodel?

  • Repairs can’t wait: a leak, a broken heater, a door that won’t close. Fix them any month, any corner.
  • Remodels are by choice: new kitchen, opening a wall, a new bathroom. In 2027, skip the North, the West, the Thái Tuế and Tuế Phá slices, and any corner where this month’s 5 sits. Can’t wait? Start in a good corner on a good day and work toward the hard one.
  • Decorating and cleaning are fine everywhere. Go easy.

Good days in 2027 for the home

Pick by your age → T43

27 “clean” days from the Vietnamese date-selection book (Trạch Nhật): no Tuế Phá, Nguyệt Phá, Tam Sát or Tứ Ly/Tứ Tuyệt, and the Đổng Công rating is very good. Dates use California time. Highlighted = best for moving in.

Two caveats. The Goat month (Jul 7 – Aug 6) repeats the whole year chart, and one book (Jianu) says to move big things out of it. Lunar month 7 (Aug 2 – Sep 1) is “ghost month” for families who keep that custom. The days still pass the Vietnamese book’s tests. Your call.

Common mistake: “The North has the 5 this year, so a North-facing home is a bad buy.” No. The year stars move every year. They tell you which corner to leave alone for a while, not which home to pass on. Check the roof, the price and the inspection first.
