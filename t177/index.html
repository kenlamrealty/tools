Next steps

  1. Build one folder: the park's letter, your rental agreement, HCD title and registration, the last 12 months of rent receipts, and photos of the home inside and out.
  2. Talk with your neighbors and form or join a residents' association. Some rights belong only to an organized group.
  3. Do not sign a buyout, a new lease or a "voluntary move-out" before legal aid reviews it.
  4. Space rent questions: T162. Is the park allowed to do this? T171. Considering a sale: T168 and T169.

A common misconception: "The park is closing, so I just have to leave." Not so. Closing a park requires city approval, a relocation impact report and months of notice. If you cannot find a space in another park, the owner may have to pay the home's in-place value, not its value as a unit hauled away. Residents who stay calm, organize and get legal help early usually come out ahead.
