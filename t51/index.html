Next steps

  1. Get real rent comps: 3 to 5 homes like yours, close by, that leased in the last 60 days. HUD figures are a floor, not your rent.
  2. Note your move-out date and the window date above. Talk with a CPA before you sign a lease. IRS references: Pub 523 (selling your home) and Pub 527 (rental property).
  3. If you rent, learn the rent cap and just-cause rules first: T55. Switch your homeowner policy to a landlord policy.
  4. Planning to sell later? Estimate the tax in T22. Want to trade into another rental instead? See T27: 1031 exchange.

A common mistake: “If the rent covers the mortgage, the house pays for itself.” Repairs, vacancy, property tax and insurance come out first. And if you sell more than 3 years after moving out, you can lose the $250K/$500K home-sale exclusion.
