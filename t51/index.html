Next steps

  1. Get real rent comps. Look at 3–5 homes like yours on your street that rented in the last 60 days. HUD numbers are a floor, not your rent.
  2. Write down your move-out date and the window date above. Talk to a CPA before you sign a lease. Read IRS Pub 523 (selling) and Pub 527 (renting).
  3. Renting? Learn the rent cap and eviction rules first: T55. Switch to a landlord insurance policy.
  4. Selling later? Check the exact tax in T22. Want to roll into another rental instead? See T27 — 1031 exchange.

Many people get this wrong: “The rent covers the mortgage, so it's free money.” Not really. Repairs, empty months, tax and insurance come first. And if you sell more than 3 years after moving out, you can lose up to $250K/$500K of tax-free gain.
