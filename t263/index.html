A common misunderstanding: "The tenants will pay my whole mortgage." In the Bay Area they rarely do. The benefit is usually more modest: you pay less than renting a comparable place, and part of each payment is principal that stays yours. Leave out repairs and vacant months and the numbers look better than they are.
