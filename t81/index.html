    Next steps

    1. Look up reports filed on this house in the past 2 years at SPCB WDO search. Ask for a copy.
    2. Check the license and the branch at search.dca.ca.gov (pick Structural Pest Control Board). Tenting needs Branch 1. Reports and wood repair: Branch 3.
    3. Big Section 1 number? Get a 2nd report and compare item by item. Then split who pays with T80.
    4. After the work, get the Notice of Work Completed (due in 10 business days, B&P §8518) before you close.

    Common mistake: Thinking any pest company can write a termite report. A Branch 2 license (ants, roaches, mice) does not cover termites or dry rot. Reports and wood repair are Branch 3. Tenting is Branch 1.
