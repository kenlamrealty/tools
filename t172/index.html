    The park and the heir

    The heir can sell the home in placeAn heir, joint tenant or estate representative can sell the home to a buyer who keeps it in the park, provided the owner's unpaid rent, utilities and reasonable maintenance under the rental agreement are paid (Civil Code §798.78).
    Unpaid rent can cost the home its spaceIf those amounts go unpaid, the park can require the home to be removed (§798.78(b)). Keep paying space rent while the title transfers.
    Living there needs separate approvalAn heir who wants to stay as a resident must meet the same approval standards as any buyer: ability to pay, past tenancy and an honest application (§798.78(d), §798.74).
    Repairs before the sale are allowedBefore the sale, the heir may repair the home to meet health and safety standards, or replace it with one that meets the park's current written standards (§798.78(c)).

    Next steps

    1. Order three to five certified death certificates. HCD, the bank and the county each want one.
    2. Call HCD Registration & Titling at 800-952-8356 with the decal number. Ask what is on file and what is owed.
    3. Tell the park in writing who is handling the home, and keep paying space rent.
    4. Planning for your own home? Compare a trust and a will in T121, and find an attorney in T122. Selling? Run T168.

    A common misconception: "No trust means probate, no way around it." For a mobile home on an HCD title, a state form avoids probate regardless of the home's value, once 40 days have passed, if nothing else in the estate requires probate (H&S Code §18102).
