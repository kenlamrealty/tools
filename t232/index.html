What to do next

    Why county sqft is not a permit

    • The assessor taxes what it sees. The building department approves what gets built. Two offices, two records.
    • Santa Clara County says its property characteristics are kept only for assessment and are not updated all the time. Alameda County says they are not part of the assessment roll.
    • So a match doesn't prove a permit, and a gap doesn't prove there's none. The permit history on the city portal does.

    Many people get this wrong: "The county shows 1,850 sqft, so all 1,850 are permitted." No. The assessor can pick up an addition from a sale or a field check and tax it, with no permit ever pulled. Lenders and buyers' appraisers look at the city record, not the tax bill.
