Many people get this wrong: "5% down works on any fourplex if I live there." Only if the loan stays at or under the baseline limit ($1,601,750 for 4 units). Above that it's a high-balance loan and Fannie wants 25% down on 3–4 units, 15% on a duplex. In Bay Area prices, that line comes fast.
