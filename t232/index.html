How to split the money
Interest saved per year is an estimate: amount paid × APR, assuming the balance would otherwise sit there for a year. Real interest depends on daily balances and new purchases. Utilization is only as good as what's on your statement closing date.

Common myth: "Paying off the biggest balance helps my score most." The score looks at utilization, total and per card. $1,000 that takes a $1,500 / $2,000 card from 75% to 25% can do more than the same $1,000 on a $9,000 balance.

Next steps

  1. Applying for a mortgage in the next 1–3 months? Use the score-first split, and pay before each statement closing date.
  2. Not buying soon? Interest first saves real money. Then set autopay so no payment is ever late.
