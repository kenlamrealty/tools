Measure your home's facing with your phone

How to measure accurately

  • Stand inside, right at the front door, back to the house, facing the street.
  • Hold the phone flat, with the top pointing straight out the door.
  • Stay 1–2 m (3–6 ft) away from cars, metal doors, the refrigerator and speakers.
  • Remove a magnetic case or MagSafe wallet.
  • If the phone asks, move it in a figure 8 to calibrate.
  • Measure three spots: the center of the front door, the center of the house, the front yard. The tool averages them.
No compass? Open Google Maps in satellite view and find the house. Read the angle the front faces (North = 0°, East = 90°). That is the true bearing. Choose “True (Google Maps)” above and the tool subtracts {d}° to get magnetic (San Jose, NOAA 11/2026).

A common misunderstanding: Measuring indoors near the refrigerator, a metal door or a car can throw the reading off by 10–20°, enough to land in another sơn and make the whole chart wrong. Stand at the door, away from metal, and measure three times.
