Before you list it

  • Rent control check (T55)
  • Deposit cap and screening fees (T52)Most landlords: max 1 month deposit since July 1, 2024. Small landlords: up to 2.
  • Lease checklist (T54)Required disclosures, move-in photos, who pays which utility.
  • Cash flow at this rent (T146)Mortgage, tax, insurance, repairs and vacancy against the rent.

Next steps

  1. Look at 5–10 similar rentals within about a mile that listed in the last 30 days. Note which ones rented fast. That is your real market.
  2. Screen every applicant the same way, in writing. In California you can't turn down Section 8 or other rent help as a source of income. Rules: CA Civil Rights Dept. · CA Landlord-Tenant Guide.
  3. Check if your unit is covered by AB 1482 or city rent rules before you sign. It decides future raises and evictions: T55 · CA Attorney General.
  4. Not sure whether to rent or sell? Run both in T51.

Many people get this wrong: “I can charge whatever the last tenant paid plus 10%.”
