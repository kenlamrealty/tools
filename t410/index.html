Your element
—

Common mistake: Painting the whole house in your element color. You don’t need to. One accent is enough: the front door, a pillow, a vase, a plant. Keep walls light and neutral. Buyers pay more for a home they can picture themselves in.

Shared palette

How it works

  • Element (mệnh) = nạp âm of your lunar birth year. Born before Tết counts as the year before.
  • Fits best: your own element and the one that feeds it (tương sinh). OK: the element you feed or control. Use lightly: the element that controls yours (tương khắc).
  • Cycle: Wood feeds Fire, Fire feeds Earth, Earth feeds Metal, Metal feeds Water, Water feeds Wood. Wood controls Earth, Earth controls Water, Water controls Fire, Fire controls Metal, Metal controls Wood.
  • Two people: colors that neither person’s element is controlled by. If one element controls the other, a “bridge” element sits between them.
