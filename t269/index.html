A common misunderstanding: "Cap rate is my return." Not if you have a loan. Cap rate ignores debt. In the Bay Area cap rates sit near 4–5% while loans cost more than 7%, so the same building can cash flow for an all-cash buyer and lose money every month for one who put 25% down. See also cash flow and DSCR.
