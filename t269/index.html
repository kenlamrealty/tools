Common mistake: "Cap rate is my return." Not if you have a loan. Cap rate ignores the mortgage. In the Bay Area cap rates sit near 4–5% while loans cost 7%+, so the same building can cash flow for an all-cash buyer and lose money every month for someone with 25% down. Look at cash flow and DSCR too.
