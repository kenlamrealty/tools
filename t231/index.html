Timing matters. Card companies usually report the balance on your statement closing date, not the due date. Pay before the closing date and the lower balance is what the bureaus see.

Common myth: "Keep a small balance on the card to build credit." You don't need to carry a balance or pay interest. Let the statement show a small amount, then pay it in full. And 30% is not a magic line: FICO says lower is better, under 10% helps.
