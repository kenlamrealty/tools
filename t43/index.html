Three best days to move in
Moving in (nhập trạch) means the first night you sleep in the new home. Good hours meet at least 2 of 3 tests from the book: hoàng đạo hour, a good Trường Sinh stage, a good Bác Sĩ star. Hours are California local time. Many families light the stove, boil water and bring in rice and salt during that hour, then move furniture whenever it suits.

A common misunderstanding: Holding out for the “perfect” day, then paying an extra month of rent or a rush moving fee. Many families hold a short ceremony on the favorable day and move the furniture on the weekend.

Each box shows the solar date, the lunar date and the day’s Trực. Tap a day for details.

How days are marked, in the book’s order. 1. Rule out Tuế Phá, Nguyệt Phá, Tứ Ly, Tứ Tuyệt, Tam Sát of the year or month, a day that clashes with your birth year (Đại Hao), Âm Sai / Dương Thác, and Trực Nguy or Bế. 2. Trực: Thành and Khai are best for moving in; Trừ, Chấp and Thu are weak. 3. 28 mansions: Thất, Tỉnh, Phòng help. 4. Đổng Công rating for the month, plus days it names for moving in. 5. Day stars (Thần Sát Trạch Nhật Cao Cấp): Thọ Tử, Tứ Phế and Vãng Vong days are ruled out; Thiên Tặc (every Trực Khai day in the Thìn, Mùi, Tuất and Sửu months), Tứ Cùng, Ngũ Mộ, Du Họa, Địa Tặc, Âm Thác and similar stars cost points and keep a day from “good” unless Thiên Đức Hợp or Nguyệt Đức Hợp (for some stars also Thiên Xá or Thiên Nguyện) cancels them. Nothing cancels Vãng Vong. Thiên Đức, Nguyệt Đức, Thiên Ân, Thiên Xá, Ngũ Hợp and Ngũ Phú add points. 6. Hours as above. Only about 1 day in 10 ends up “good”.
