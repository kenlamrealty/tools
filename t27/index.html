0/0 done

A common misunderstanding: "I can 1031 the home I live in." No. A 1031 applies only to real property held for investment or business use. A primary residence falls under a different rule (§121): up to $250K / $500K of gain excluded if you qualify. See T22.

Next steps

  1. Before you list, meet with a CPA and bring your depreciation records. The basis figure drives every result above.
  2. Choose a Qualified Intermediary and sign the exchange agreement before the sale closes. Sale proceeds should never pass through your account.
  3. Get pre-approved for the replacement now (T34). 45 days go quickly.
  4. Buying outside California? File FTB 3840 every year until the deferred gain is taxed.
