0/0 done

Common mistake: "I can 1031 my own home." No. A 1031 is only for property held for investment or business. Your primary home uses a different rule: up to $250K / $500K of gain tax-free. See T22.

Next steps

  1. Before you list, call a CPA with your depreciation records. The basis number decides everything above.
  2. Pick a Qualified Intermediary and sign the exchange agreement before the sale closes. Never let sale money touch your account.
  3. Get a loan pre-approval for the replacement now. 45 days goes fast.
  4. Buying outside California? File FTB 3840 every year until you sell.
