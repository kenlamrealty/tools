Next steps

  1. Buying in a park? Call the lender first and ask whether the park is on its approved list. Then get the park's rental agreement so the lender can review the lease term.
  2. Pull the home's HCD title record and the HUD label or data plate number. Year built and number of sections decide most of this list. Not sure? T166.
  3. On your own land and want a 30-year mortgage? The home must sit on a permanent foundation and be converted to real property with HCD form 433A. See T174.
  4. Get two or three real quotes, then enter the payment in T145 with space rent, tax and insurance for the full monthly number.

A common misconception: "I'll just get an FHA loan." The standard 30-year FHA loan (Title II) requires land you own and a permanent foundation. In a park, the options are almost always chattel or FHA Title I, and only a few lenders offer Title I.
