SB 800: the new-home defect law

California's "Right to Repair Act." It lists what a new home must not do (leak, crack, short out), how long you have to claim, and the steps you and the builder must follow before anyone goes to court.

Who gives it
The builder, with the original sales papers (§912(g))
Who signs
The buyer initials that they got a copy; so does the builder's sales rep
When
Buying a newly built home from a builder, and later if something goes wrong
Required?
Yes. It's state law for new homes sold under contracts signed on or after 1/1/2003 (§938)
Pages
About 20 pages: Civil Code §§895–945.5, five chapters.
