Common mistake: "It's all closing costs, so the QI can pay it." Not quite. A simple test many QIs use: would this cost exist if you paid all cash? Loan fees, points and the lender appraisal would not, so they are usually treated as boot. Many investors bring a small check to closing for those instead.
