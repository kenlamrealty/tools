Next steps

  1. Ask for the full disclosure package before you write an offer, not after. Use T151.
  2. Read the TDS and SPQ next to the inspection reports. If the seller says “no leaks” and the report shows water stains, ask why. T71 helps you read reports.
  3. Fire or flood zone on the NHD? Get an insurance quote before you remove contingencies. Help: CA Dept. of Insurance.
  4. Check sex-offender info yourself at meganslaw.ca.gov. The seller doesn't do it for you.
  1. Start the TDS and SPQ the week you list, while you remember. T23 has the full prep checklist.
  2. Order the NHD, termite and a home inspection before you go live. Buyers trust a home with reports up front.
  3. Thinking “as-is” or cash? Compare your options with T26. You still disclose.
  4. East Bay city? Book the sewer lateral test early. It can hold up your closing.

Many people get this wrong: “The inspector will find everything, so the seller doesn't have to say much.” Not true. The seller must tell you what they know, even if an inspector could find it. And an inspector can't see a leak that was fixed with paint last month.
