General guide for California, 2026 numbers. Family farms, irrevocable trusts and estates over $15M have extra rules. Ask a CPA or an estate attorney before you sign a deed.

Common mistake: "Adding my child's name to the deed saves probate, so it's the safe move." It can cost far more than probate. The share you give keeps your old basis, so your child loses the step-up on it and may owe capital gains on decades of growth when they sell. A living trust usually avoids probate without that cost.
