Next step

  1. Not sure of the profit before depreciation? Run rent, expenses and loan in T146.
  2. Pull the property tax bill. The Land vs Improvements split there is the number your CPA will likely use.
  3. Thinking of selling later? See how a 1031 exchange defers the recapture: T222.
  4. Rents below market? See how fast the law lets you catch up: T266.

Many people get this wrong: "My rental shows a loss, so it cuts my W-2 tax." Only if your MAGI is under $150,000 (and fully only under $100,000). Above that the loss is parked, not gone. And depreciation you skip still gets taxed when you sell, because the IRS counts what was "allowable."
