Suitable for what?

A common misunderstanding: Thinking the day you sign is the day the home becomes yours. It is not. After you sign the offer, escrow runs for the period set in your contract, and the home is yours on the day the deed records. If you want a favorable day, use it for signing the offer or the loan documents. Do not let a contingency deadline slip while you wait for one.

How it works

  • In the order of the Trạch Nhật book (Nguyễn Thành Phương): 1. rule out 7 kinds of bad days — Tuế Phá, Nguyệt Phá, Tứ Ly, Tứ Tuyệt, Tam Sát, Đại Hao (birth-year clash), Nhật Phá (hours). 2. score the 12 Trực. 3. the 28 mansions. 4. task-specific stars and the Đổng Công table. 5. pick the hour.
  • Signing, buying, selling: Kiếp Sát days are ruled out (the book says avoid large money transactions). Trực Thành, Khai, Định, Thu are good; Trực Mãn works against the buyer (Trạch Nhật book) and for the seller — the Thần Sát book calls Mãn “Thiên Phú”, good for contracts. Trực Kiến: the Trạch Nhật book rates it fair, the Thần Sát book says avoid contracts, so the tool no longer adds points for it. Mansions Trương, Chẩn, Tất, Bích help; Liễu, Cang, Ngưu, Tỉnh hurt.
  • Moving in: also rules out Tam Sát, Âm Sai / Dương Thác, Trực Nguy and Bế; Thành and Khai are best. Breaking ground: Tam Sát is an absolute no. Month and year pillars, the Trực and hoàng đạo follow solar terms (the year turns at Lập Xuân), not the lunar calendar.
  • Lunar dates are shown in the Vietnamese calendar (UTC+7). Folk taboos are labels only. Contract and escrow deadlines always come first.
  • Day stars (Thần Sát Trạch Nhật Cao Cấp): Thọ Tử and Tứ Phế days are ruled out (Vãng Vong too for moving and building). Stars the book names for each task cost points and keep a day from “Good”: signing — Thiên Tặc, Tứ Cùng, Cửu Không, Tứ Hao, Ương Bại, Tiểu Hao; moving in — Thiên Tặc, Tứ Cùng, Ngũ Mộ, Du Họa, Địa Tặc, Âm Thác; groundwork — Thổ Phủ, Địa Nang, Thiên Địa Chuyển; openings — Cửu Không, Tứ Hao, Ương Bại, Địa Tặc. Thiên Đức Hợp and Nguyệt Đức Hợp (for some stars also Thiên Đức, Nguyệt Đức, Thiên Xá, Thiên Nguyện) cancel them; nothing cancels Vãng Vong. Thiên Đức, Nguyệt Đức, Thiên Ân, Thiên Xá, Nguyệt Ân, Ngũ Phú and Ngũ Hợp add points.
