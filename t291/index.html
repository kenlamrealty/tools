    What each status means
    
    
      
  
      

  
      
    
      Many people get this wrong: "There's a permit, so it's legal." Only Finaled means the final inspection passed. Issued means the work was allowed to start. Expired usually means nobody came back for the final. To a buyer's appraiser, an expired addition can read a lot like no permit at all.
      
    
      One more: A remodel permit doesn't add square feet. A kitchen permit from 2010 says nothing about the family room behind the kitchen.
      
    
      And this one: "Appraisers give unpermitted space zero value." That's a habit, not a rule. Fannie Mae tells the appraiser to comment on the quality of the work and its effect, if any, on value. That's why this tool says "at risk", not "lost".
