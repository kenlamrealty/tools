      Documents to bring

        Check the license before you sign

        • Look up the license at cslb.ca.gov (License Check). Confirm active status, a bond on file and workers' comp on file.
        • The $1,000 rule (since January 1, 2025): no license is needed only when the entire job is under $1,000 (labor plus materials), no workers are hired, AND no permit is required. If it needs a permit, it needs a license, however small.
        • New for 2026: the home improvement contract must name any subcontractor who will perform more than half of the project cost, and a homeowner who cancels within the 3-day right to cancel may do so by email.

        A common mistake: "The contractor said to pull it as owner-builder because it's cheaper." That makes you the employer: payroll, workers' comp (with fines cited up to $100,000), and the cost if someone is hurt. A contractor who asks for this often has no license.
