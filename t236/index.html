Many people get this wrong: "Rents are $600 under market, so I'll raise them after closing." Under San Jose ARO that's 5% a year. If market rent grows 3% a year, you close the gap by about 2% a year. A 25% gap can take more than 10 years. Pay for the rent roll you get, not the one you hope for.
