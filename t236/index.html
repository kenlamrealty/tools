Which score does the lender use?

Rule: each person's middle of 3 scores (or the lower of 2). With 2 borrowers, Fannie Mae and FHA use the lowest borrower's score. Fannie Mae's average median score is now used only for manual underwriting (620 fixed / 640 ARM); DU no longer has a minimum. A lender may pick Classic FICO or VantageScore 4.0, but one model for everyone on the loan.

Common myth: "The lender uses the higher score, or our average." No. For pricing, the lender takes each person's middle score, then uses the lower of the two. A strong co-borrower doesn't pull the weaker score up.
