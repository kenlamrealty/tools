Next steps

  1. Confirm the year the building got its certificate of occupancy (city permit records). One year can change which law applies.
  2. Give the notice in writing: 30 days for 10% or less, 90 days for more. If you mail it, add 5 days.
  4. Own a single-family home or condo as an individual? Put the AB 1482 exemption notice in the lease, word for word from Civil Code §1947.12(d)(5).

Many people get this wrong: “single-family homes are always exempt.” Only if the owner isn't a corporation, REIT or LLC with a corporate member, and the tenant got the written exemption notice. No notice, no exemption.

General information, not legal advice. Rules change and every building has its own history (banked increases, petitions, registration). Confirm with the city rent program or a landlord-tenant attorney before you send a notice.
