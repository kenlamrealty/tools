Estimate. Who pays is custom, not law: it is negotiable in the contract. Escrow and title compute the final number.

When is it $0?

  • R&T 11911 Gift, no money paidTax is on consideration only. No price, no tax. Taking over the loan can count as consideration.
  • R&T 11930 Inheritance, or into / out of a trust with no saleWrite "R&T 11930, transfer to trust, no consideration" on the deed for your own living trust.
  • R&T 11927 Spouses in a divorce or legal separationDeed between spouses under a divorce or separation.
  • R&T 11925 Into your own LLC, same % ownershipSame people, same shares before and after. Change the shares later and tax can come back.

Common mistake: "Transfer tax is a small fee." In Santa Clara County it is, about $1.10 per $1,000. Cross into San Jose above $2.3M, or Oakland and Berkeley, and it jumps to tens of thousands. The rate also applies to the whole price, not just the part above the line. $1 over can cost more than $17,000.
