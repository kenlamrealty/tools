The mold booklet for renters

A 4-page state booklet your landlord must hand you before you sign a lease. It says: damp and mold are bad for your lungs, here is how to spot them, and the owner has to fix them.

Who gives it
The landlord or property manager
Who signs
No signature on the booklet itself. Many leases add a line saying you got it.
When
Before you sign a rental or lease agreement
Required?
Yes, for residential landlords since 1/1/2022 (Health & Safety Code §26148)
Pages
4 pages. Made by the California Department of Public Health (CDPH), 2020, English and Spanish.
