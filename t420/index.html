ACS: how two agents split a commission

A paper two agents sign so they agree up front: who gets what share of the pay, who covers costs, and whose clients are whose if they split up.

4 pagesSigned by 2 agents + broker(s)When two agents team up

What does this form say to me?

Type one deal. The split updates as you type. The form splits the gross pay between the two agents first, then each agent splits with their own broker (para 4).
