Paperwork: file BOE-19-P with the county assessor within 3 years of the transfer, or before the house goes to anyone else, whichever comes first. The child also files the homeowners' exemption (BOE-266) within 1 year of moving in. Late filing only helps going forward.
Estimate. The cap $1,044,586 is for transfers from 02/16/2025 to 02/15/2027; it changes again in February 2027. Bonds and parcel taxes differ by city. The county assessor makes the final call.

Common mistake: "My parents' low tax passes to me automatically." Not since Feb 16, 2021. The child must make it their own home within a year, and only about $1.04M of value above the old base is protected. Plan to rent it out? It's reassessed at full market value, even if the parents lived there 40 years.
