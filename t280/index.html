    A permit, like you're 10

    A permit is the city saying "OK, you can build this, and we'll come check it." You show the plan, pay a fee, then an inspector comes by at key moments: before the walls close, and at the end. The last sign-off is called the final. No final, no finish line. It's like a school project: turning it in is not enough, the teacher has to grade it.

    The 5 steps of every permit

      How long a permit stays alive
      California: work must start within 12 months of the issue date (CRC R105.5.1, H&S §18938.6). After that, if work stops for more than 180 days between inspections, the permit can expire. Extensions are up to 180 days each, in writing, with a reason. Oakland is stricter: a major inspection every 6 months, and the whole job finished within 2 years. Each city adds its own rules. Check the dates in T285.
